Transfer characterization: how heterogeneous are contexts, and why?

cps / gss / cfps · time and group (ethnicity) families · analyst-side, reads A and B. Q5 (a learned composition model) is not built: with ~8 pairs we stay below the corpus threshold to learn transport, so it remains a corpus-gated follow-on.

Q1 — composition vs mechanism

Per outcome, the share of the A→B gap explained by reweighting A's demographics to B's (composition); the remainder is mechanism. Above the dashed line = composition-dominated.

Q2 — X-composition distance

Mean distance between the two contexts' demographic marginals; larger = the populations differ more in who they contain.

Q3 — mechanism stability

Fraction of variable-pairs whose association is stable vs shifted between contexts (|Δ| threshold 0.10). More 'stable' = the dependence structure transfers.

Q4 — shape vs level

For numeric outcomes, whether the conditional curve moved by a constant offset (level, cheaply correctable) or changed slope (shape, needs real adaptation).
