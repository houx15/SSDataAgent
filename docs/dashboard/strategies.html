Does an LLM agent discover this strategy on its own?

The ~0.50 wall — and why it's a wall, not a weak method

Every no-donor method lands at the same place. Most tellingly, an oracle fed the true correlations reaches the same 0.50 as the best LLM — so ~0.50 is the information limit for anything with marginals but no microdata. The gap up to 0.806 is higher-order joint structure only real rows carry.

Published LLMs sit below the trivial floor because they fail the marginals. Handing over the aggregate stats — zero intelligence — is the biggest no-donor win; the LLM's job is only the joint structure on top, and there it hits ~0.50.
